# Bambora taste profile: what a confident, watchable yapper looks like

Built on 2026-09-23 from 30 **yapper-style** approved videos (straight to camera for at least part of the video) by Fatima's 10 loving creators (Cambria Reau, Cassie Avery Charvat, Tasha Clay, Tayler Raza, Sophia Lease, Lilly Clark, Myrka Bustillo, and others) plus her 30 most recent approvals (Jennifer Thomas, Ali Fannin, Brittany Archutowski, Georgie Alex, Cloe Peluola, Aubrie Martinez, McKinzey Cline and others). Non-yapper videos were excluded on purpose, per Fatima. Data: `2026-09-23/taste_rows.json`, script: `scripts/taste_metrics.py`.

## The numbers (first 25 seconds)
| Measure | Winners' typical range (p25 to p75) | Median | Read it as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speaking pace | 3.4 to 4.4 words/sec | 3.9 | Brisk and fluent. Below about 3.0 usually means hesitant or rambling |
| Talk coverage | 90% to 100% of the clip | 98% | They talk nearly the whole time. No long silent b-roll stretches |
| First words | within 0.1 to 0.25 sec | 0.15 s | No dead air. The hook starts immediately |
| Loudness variation | 2.9 to 3.7 dB | 3.4 | Steady, controlled delivery. Not shouty, not flat |
| Pitch variation | 42 to 55 Hz | 46 | Natural, conversational expressiveness |

## What you see in winning frames
- The **baby is on her in the carrier** while she talks, in a real home, porch, yard or kitchen. Real life, not a studio.
- She **looks into the lens** and keeps talking while moving: adjusting the sling, walking, bouncing baby.
- Big, natural expressions, but the confidence is in the steadiness, not the volume. Calm-and-warm (Sophia Lease) and bubbly (Ali Fannin, Jennifer Thomas) both win.
- Text-overlay hooks on screen are common alongside the talking.

## How to score an applicant
1. Are they yapper (speaking to camera at some point)? If not, the energy question doesn't apply.
2. Measure their featured or TikTok videos with the same script. **Watchable** = pace of about 3.0 or more, talking for most of the clip, starting fast. Within or near the winners' range = confident.
3. Look at the frames: eye contact, easy expression, comfortable on camera. Red flags: reading or stiff, trailing off, long pauses, looking away, mumbling.
4. Numbers are a guide, not a gate. A calm, slower speaker who is clearly composed can still pass. Say why.
