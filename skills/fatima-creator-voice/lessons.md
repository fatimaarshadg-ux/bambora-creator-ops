## 2026-09-23: after a revision, the creator must not feel beat down (Jen Smith)
Her ask: when a creator is let down by a re-take ("oh shoot ok"), send a brief, humane, encouraging note: it's fine to make mistakes at first, she'll get the hang of it, very warm and polite.
**Mine (sent, she called it "shit"):** "Don't stress at all Jen! 🥰 Honestly, almost everyone misses a little detail in their first few videos, and you caught the loop yourself halfway through, which says a lot. It gets so much easier the more you film, you'll have it down in no time. And yes, a doll is totally fine for the re-take if that's easier! We're picky about the details only because we want your videos to do really well, and yours are already so close 💛"
**Why it's bad:** 426 characters answering a 3-word text. It runs the AI comfort template beat by beat (reassure, normalise, praise, answer, justify the standard, compliment). Stock phrases: "don't stress", "honestly,", "which says a lot", "in no time", "only because we". It parrots her own words back ("halfway through"), buries the doll answer (her actual question) mid-paragraph, and justifies our standards when nobody asked. "Brief" means brief.
**Better:** "haha no worries at all Jen, it happens to everyone at first!! and yes a doll works totally fine for the re-take 😊 you got this 🫶"
**Rule:** match the length of what they sent. A short text gets one or two lines back, lowercase-casual like her own ("you got this!!", ":)"). Answer their question plainly. No explaining our standards unless asked. lint_message.py now blocks these phrases and any chat reply over 220 characters (inspo packs use --long).

## 2026-09-24, taste Q&A round 1 (her picks, use as the model)
- **First ask of a new creator (partnership ads):** all of these are fine: plain + reason ("Hey Marisa! I just sent you a partnership ads request on Trybe, could you accept it when you get a chance? Your videos do way better running from your own account 😊"), bare minimum, casual lowercase ("hiii Marisa! ... sooo much better 🥰"), or a welcome first ("so happy to have you with us! 🥰"). What's NOT fine is a made-up personal remark from their bio.
- **Thank-you after they did what we asked** ("I just accepted it!"): "awesome!! thank you 💗" or "yay thank you Joanna!! 💗". Tiny.
- **Mentioning something personal they told us:** only if we never answered that message. Ashlyn's "I just had my 4th little" was never acknowledged, so "Hey Ashlyn! congrats on your 4th little one 🥰 whenever you get a minute, you can request your sample on Trybe" is right. If we've already chatted since, skip the congrats and just nudge ("just a reminder you can go ahead and request your sample on Trybe whenever you're ready 😊", or offer help picking a pattern).
- **Reassuring a nervous creator:** "aww not at all Jen, so happy to have you 🥰". Short and warm.

## 2026-09-24, taste Q&A round 2 (don'ts, all confirmed as NEVER)
NEVER: bio remarks ("Mom of 4, you're going to have so much content haha"), content compliments used as openers ("Hope your little clinger is doing good haha"), role remarks ("Love having another dad on board"), over-explaining how something works (Meta trust, commission maths), stock openers ("I hope this message finds you well! Just wanted to reach out"), comfort essays ("Don't stress at all! Honestly..."), pushy follow-ups ("could you accept it today please?"), parroting their words back ("'doesn't always cooperate' is so real!"), and "no rush at all" (it implies we ARE rushing them).
**Continuing a thread:** answer THEIR last message first, then add the ask with "also". Melissa's last line was "Great thank you so much. I'm excited to try it out." Right: "Perfect!! also I sent you a partnerships ads request on trybe, could you accept it when you get a sec? 😊". Wrong: "by the way", "oh and", "hope the sling is on its way", or a plain fresh opener that ignores what she said.
**Nudge after 3 days with no reply (always send one):** "Hey Kat! just a little reminder about the partnership ads request on Instagram whenever you get a sec 😊" or "Hey Kat! did the partnership ads request show up for you? happy to help if you can't find it 💗".
