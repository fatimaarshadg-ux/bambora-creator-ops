# Angle craft — the proven hook set and how to use it

## The concept / angle test

Run every idea through this before writing:

> **Does this give the customer a reason to buy?**

| Idea | Reason to buy? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "I want to test a video version" | No | Concept — find the angle |
| "I want to test Us vs Them" | No | Concept — us vs them on *what*? |
| "I want to do an iteration" | No | Concept — iteration of *which* angle? |
| "Stops tossing and turning" | Yes — solves their problem | **Angle** |
| "Brings back your energy" | Yes | **Angle** |
| "I want to test buy it for life" | Yes | **Angle** (concept and angle coincide) |

Concepts are internally focused — about your test strategy. Angles are
externally focused — about their reason to buy.

## The proven hook set

Sixteen angle/hook pairs from ads that worked. Use them as **patterns**, not
templates — the job is to identify the structural move and rebuild it for the
brand in front of you.

| Angle | Hook |
|---|---|
| Stops swelling | "14+ Hours. No Swelling" |
| Protein shake keeps you full | "This is a meal. Not a protein shake" |
| Don't sacrifice what you like to be healthy | "Don't change your life, just change lunch" |
| Sleeping better | "Sleep like a baby" |
| Getting rid of double chin | "Drains double chin in 7 days" |
| Nutella turns bread into breakfast | "This is bread. This is breakfast" |
| Take control of your life with better food | "Grab life by the bowls" |
| Losing weight without trying | "I accidentally lost 12 pounds at 52" |
| Losing hormonal weight | "I flushed 19 pounds of hormonal weight" |
| Dealing with a grumpy teenager | "Teen always grumpy?" |
| Showing people they have wavy hair | "3 signs your hair is actually wavy" |
| Tick preventatives don't work | "Ever wonder why tick preventatives don't work anymore?" |
| Vitamin deficiency in kids | "3 red flags of vitamin deficiency in kids" |
| Shampoo with better ingredients | "Watch for these ingredients when buying shampoo" |
| A wallet that's RFID proof | "Why millennials are ditching their old wallets" |

### The patterns underneath

**Timeline + eliminated outcome** — "14+ Hours. No Swelling", "Drains double
chin in 7 days". Specific duration paired with a specific thing that stops
happening. The number does the believing.

**Redefinition** — "This is a meal. Not a protein shake", "This is bread. This
is breakfast". Refuses the product's obvious category and asserts a better one.
This is `evolve-new-mechanism`'s anti-categorisation rule expressed as a hook.

**Minimal change, maximum result** — "Don't change your life, just change
lunch". Removes the effort objection before it's raised.

**Accidental achievement** — "I accidentally lost 12 pounds at 52". Effort-free
plus specific plus age-identified. Powerful because it defeats the "I've tried
everything" objection without arguing.

**Numbered diagnostic** — "3 signs your hair is actually wavy", "3 red flags of
vitamin deficiency in kids". Promises self-knowledge, not a product. Best on
audiences who don't yet know they have the problem.

**Direct avatar call-out** — "Teen always grumpy?". Names the person's exact
situation. Crude and highly effective on a specific sub-avatar.

**Contrarian question** — "Ever wonder why tick preventatives don't work
anymore?". Validates a private suspicion. Strong on burned audiences who've tried
competitors.

**Insider warning** — "Watch for these ingredients when buying shampoo". Positions
the brand as an advisor rather than a seller.

**Herd movement** — "Why millennials are ditching their old wallets". Social
proof plus curiosity, without naming a benefit at all.

## Worked output

The shape to follow. Note the ranking, the pattern attribution on every hook,
and the two priority notes that top and tail the document.

> ## Marketing Angles & Hooks
> ### Total Blackout Blinds — Restorative Sleep Avatar
>
> **PRIORITY NOTE:** You do NOT have to test each angle separately. Test the #1
> hook from Sub-Avatar #3 first — it carries the most emotional weight. You can
> layer supporting benefits from other angles into body copy.
>
> ---
>
> ### SUB-AVATAR #3: Short Fuse Avatar ⭐ STRONGEST
>
> **Angle 1: Stops You From Snapping At People**
> **Reason to Buy:** Complete darkness lets your nervous system actually
> recover, so you stop being irritable with the people you care about.
> **Hook:** "5 minutes to install. No more snapping."
> **Inspired by:** "14+ Hours. No Swelling" — timeline + specific outcome elimination
>
> **Angle 2: Restores Your Patience**
> **Reason to Buy:** Your brain needs pitch-black sleep to reset your nervous
> system. Get that, get your patience back.
> **Hook:** "This blocks light. This saves relationships."
> **Inspired by:** "This is bread. This is breakfast" — the "This does X. This does Y" pattern
>
> **Angle 3: Eliminates The "On Edge" Feeling**
> **Reason to Buy:** Stop feeling wired and irritable all day by giving your body
> the complete darkness it needs to repair while you sleep.
> **Hook:** "Seals 100% of light. Restores 100% of patience."
> **Inspired by:** "14+ Hours. No Swelling" — percentage + outcome promise
>
> ---
>
> ### SUB-AVATAR #1: Cool Sleeper ⭐ SECOND STRONGEST
>
> **Angle 1: Completes Your Cool Sleep Setup**
> **Reason to Buy:** You already have the fan and the AC. This is the missing
> piece that blocks heat and light so the room stays cool and dark all night.
> **Hook:** "You have the fan. You have the AC. You need this."
> **Inspired by:** "Don't change your life, just change lunch" — minimal change framing
>
> ---
>
> **TESTING PRIORITY:** Start with Sub-Avatar #3, Angle 1. Test that hook first.
> If it hits, run variations. If not, move to Sub-Avatar #1, Angle 1.

## Quality bar

- Every angle states a reason to buy a customer would recognise as their own
- Every hook is attributed to the pattern it was built from
- The document is ranked, and the ranking is argued
- The priority note tells the user they can test multiple angles in one concept
- No angle is a restatement of a product feature
- Nothing broad enough to fit a competitor's product equally well
