# Decision guide: do I act, or ask Fatima?

Use this page whenever you are not sure who decides. It comes from Fatima's own rules (memory `core-rules`, `feedback-take-ownership`, `creator-response-cadence`, `bambora-creator-acceptance-criteria`, `trybe-sample-request-workflow`, `inspo-message-rules`, `bambora-protected-creators`, `creator-base-retainer-offer`, `feedback-ask-before-guessing` and the skills).

## The four rules behind every answer

1. **A rule covers it and it can be undone:** Claude does it in this sweep and tells you after. You don't need to say anything.
2. **It cannot be undone** (approve or reject a video, accept or reject an applicant, a first-time DM that needs a go): Claude brings it to you with a recommendation. **You** say yes or no.
3. **Money, the 10 protected creators, program settings, a new Trybe key:** check with **Fatima** before you say yes. Claude gives you a ready draft to show her.
4. **Not sure?** Say **"hold 3"** (or whichever number). Nothing breaks by waiting one sweep. Ask Claude "why?" or "show me" as often as you like.

Answering tip: "yes 1 and 3, no 2, hold 4". Claude acts on the yeses straight away.

## Who decides: 38 real examples

**Legend:** **Claude** = Claude does it on its own, tells you after. **You** = Claude asks you; you decide. **Fatima** = you check with Fatima first. **Nobody** = leave it alone, no reply.

### Everyday messages

| # | Situation | Who | What happens |

| 1 | A creator writes "thank you so much, so excited!!" | Claude | Heart reaction plus a short warm line in Fatima's voice. Never skipped. |
| 2 | A creator asks "can I use it with my 8 lb newborn?" | Claude | Answers from the weight rule: 10 to 50 lbs, so wait until baby is 10 lbs, or film a no-baby format (a tester review, "the gift I'm saving"). Never suggests using it from birth. |

| 4 | A ledger follow-up is due (a check-in, "what did you think of the inspo?") | Claude | Sends it in this sweep. It never comes to you as a "to do" list. |

| 6 | A creator asks a question Claude cannot answer from the rules | You | It comes in "Questions" with a draft reply. Say "send it", or tell Claude what to change. |
| 7 | A creator says "the links in the brief aren't working" (anything about Fatima's own files, links or promises that isn't 100% clear) | You, then Fatima if you aren't sure either | Claude asks one line with its best guess.
