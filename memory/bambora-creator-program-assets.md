  `~/Bombara/bambora-content-checklist.html`, single self-contained file with photos
  embedded as data URIs. Also an Artifact at claude.ai/artifact/QsYAmoAo9UkiWSXjo4RvHs.

  winning videos with safety flags.
- **Renamed video set:** `~/Bombara/top-performers/` (01-15), mirrored in the Google Drive
  folder "Bambora Creator Brief" (id `1QRX-mhJiKirwNOznaYGOwC8Fqo7BsI3_`).
