Fatima's retainer model (stated 2026-09-25 while answering Maddie Tesimale):
- Creators who keep up good volume (20 to 30 videos a month) consistently for 30 to 60 days get brought on with a guaranteed **base retainer of $400 a month**.
- Framing: the retainer is peace of mind (they'll make at least that much); "the real magic is in the commissions". Mention the figure so expectations stay realistic.
- Her pitch line on volume: creators who post 20 to 30 videos a month usually hit at least 2 to 3 winners. Weekly inspo (based on top performers in the ad account) is what makes that volume easy; creators can replicate closely or add their own spin.
- There is no deadline and no per-product video quota.
- Don't mention the separate "$300 base at $1,000 GMV in 30 days" idea she floated and then dropped.

This is different from the ignore-list retainer askers in [[core-rules]] (creators demanding a retainer up front). Related: [[fatima-creator-voice]], [[trybe-5pct-migration-followup]].
