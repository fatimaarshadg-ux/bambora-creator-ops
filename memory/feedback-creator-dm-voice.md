Her corrections on 2026-09-23 after I drafted "personalised" applicant DMs:

- **Never repeat their exact words back.** Example to avoid, for Bailey: "Carrying your son while pushing the stroller is such a real mom moment". That was her own pitch sentence mirrored back, and it's the classic AI tell. A real person either shortens or tweaks the detail into something casual, or leaves it out.
- **Read the room.** If their pitch is generic (clearly sent to every brand, like Michelle Thode's "lifestyle and motherhood content creator... humor, encouragement..."), don't write a personalised reply. Flattering a template reads as AI answering AI. Personalise only when they gave something genuinely specific.
- **Don't infer traits you haven't seen.** I called Michelle's content "humour" from a word in her pitch, but her videos don't show it.
- **Plain template is better than fake personalisation.** Her preferred Bailey message was just: "Hey Bailey, thank you so much for applying with us! Do you have any yapper style / talking head content? We've seen that format do really well for us 💙"
- **Compliment only what's genuinely complimentable.** Don't praise an ordinary video ("Loved your water bottle video, you're so natural"). Over-praising cheapens her praise and sounds salesy or AI. Neutral acknowledgement shows you looked. Her Cammy example: "Hey Cammy, thank you for applying with us! I saw your water bottle video. Could you share more talking head videos, or your TikTok or Instagram? Those videos do really well for us 💙"
- **Spot AI-written pitches.** An em dash (or the polished "just looking at it as a mama" style) in an applicant's message usually means AI helped write it, so treat it as generic. React lightly like a person would ("haha, very happy to hear that"), never by quoting their line back.
- **Label style examples clearly** as "example only, not sending". On 2026-09-23 she saw the already-sent plain DMs in threads and thought hypothetical examples had gone out, or were about to. Nothing was duplicated: the send routine skips threads that already contain the message and confirms the count is exactly 1 after sending.
- Structure she approved: warm thanks, one clear ask, a friendly reason (coaching, not gatekeeping), 💙, no sign-off, no "Bambora".

Related review lessons from the same conversation:
- **Flag "no social proof" applicants explicitly** (no featured videos, no socials, nothing in their message to judge). Don't recommend a warm DM off the back of one nice pitch.
- **Numbers without yapper content need more scrutiny.** Check whether their own videos (not their liked videos) show anything talking-head.
- She also checks whether the creator actually has a baby or toddler who fits the carrier (for example, a pregnancy in their profile photo). Ask her whether this should be a formal criterion.
