Her rules for personalised inspo DMs (stated 2026-09-23, evening):

- **Order of work:** handle incoming submissions and creator messages first, then inspo.
- **Approval:** during the 2026-09-23 run (her words: "this run"), every inspo message goes to her before sending, because she wants Claude to learn her style. Log each edit in fatima-creator-voice lessons.md. Ask her before sending inspo unreviewed in later runs.
- **Research each creator first:** watch their past videos (frame sheets plus transcripts in creator-db), read how they've talked to her in DMs, and look at their Instagram/TikTok style. Recommendations must be specific to that creator.
- **Every inspo message must say:**
  1. These are only inspiration; they can do whatever they like with them and put their own spin on it. Creators who add their own spin to their ads do really well.
  2. The safety guidelines / checklist link: https://bamborachecklist.netlify.app/
  3. The safety part goes at the very END as a P.S., in her words: "P.S. We've updated our safety guidelines, so I've made a checklist you can follow while making your videos: https://bamborachecklist.netlify.app/" (her call on 2026-09-23; no separate safety paragraph in the body). **Never repeat that exact P.S. to someone who already got it** (her rule, 2026-09-23): the next time, say it the way a person would ("and here's the checklist again, just in case 😊", "the checklist is still here if you need a refresher"), and vary the wording between creators too. Check the thread for an earlier checklist link before writing.
  4. A warm close like "hope you like it, and just know I'm always here to answer any questions you may have". Vary it per creator.
- **No baby to wear yet (pregnant, or a newborn under 10 lbs): use the tester or authority angle (her note, 2026-09-23).** They position themselves as someone who has tested lots of baby products: "I've tried all of these, here's the one I'd buy", rate it out of 10, honest carrier reviews, a "second time mom here" authority hook, the sling shown on its own or in a comparison. No baby is needed in the shot. Pack examples: wearmybaby expert review, aprilnlynch carrier review, thebabygearconsultant picks, and Atria 34/48 (podcast rating out of 10) and 35 (expert authority hook). Applies to Sophia Lease, Katherine Bodie, Chelsea Debruin, Aubri Tranel and anyone else pregnant.
- **Reaction / stitch format for pregnant creators and newborns under 10 lbs (Fatima's idea, 2026-09-23, from Katherine's video):** react to or stitch a popular online video of a mom complaining about her "Velcro baby" or clingy baby, then pitch the sling in STORY form: "my sister used this with her clingy one, so I'm getting it too", "this is what I'm doing differently this time". No baby is needed in the shot, so it fits alongside the tester, rating and authority angles.
- **More no-baby angles (Fatima, 2026-09-23):** a pregnancy shopping haul with the sling as a must-have; "the best baby shower gift I got" (or "the gift I'm asking for"); a registry must-haves list; a "what I'm doing differently this time" video for second-time moms. **NOT a hospital bag pack** (Fatima, 2026-09-23): it implies using the sling straight after birth, when the baby is under 10 lbs. Never suggest or imply using the sling from birth, day one or "coming home from the hospital". Frame newborn-stage ideas as "for when they're a bit bigger (10 lbs+)".
- **"The gift I'm saving" angle (Fatima, 2026-09-23):** for pregnant creators and newborns under 10 lbs, pitch the sling as a gift ("my mom got me this", "my favorite baby shower gift") that she's saving for when the baby hits 10 lbs: "as soon as he's 10 lbs, this is what I'm using". It's honest, story-shaped, and inside the weight rule. This replaces any hospital bag framing.
- **Every reaction or stitch suggestion must include a specific video to react to** (a real link to a popular clingy or Velcro baby complaint video, from tags.tsv format=reaction_source, or found fresh). Find those links LAST, after the rest of the inspo message is written, because it takes the longest.
- **Come up with angles yourself (Fatima, 2026-09-23: "you should also know how to come up with these ideas").** For each creator, start from their life stage and moments (pregnant, newborn under 10 lbs, toddler, twins, big family, dad, grandparent, working mom), then ask: where does a sling naturally show up in their week (shopping, a baby shower, packing, errands, outings, chores, travel), what do they complain about or get asked, and what story could they tell about it? Turn each answer into a filmable format (haul, reaction, story, list, comparison, day in the life, "gift" reveal, POV). Use the inspo pack as examples of each format, not as the only ideas.
- **Never the same message to everyone.** Warm, specific, her voice ([[feedback-creator-dm-voice]]).
- **Links:** the Drive "Bambora Inspo" folder (id 1HleYAep0n7C5gFGt3RhRsgy0dmiQEb8Y) has been shared as anyone-with-the-link viewer since 2026-09-23, so Drive file links (https://drive.google.com/file/d/<id>/view) work for creators. Ids for each week's files are in ~/claude-setup/work/inspo/<date>/driveids.txt.
- **Who gets inspo (her rule, 2026-09-23):** only creators who already HAVE the product (established creators with the sling, or new creators whose sample has arrived). New creators still waiting on their sample get NO inspo; their next touch is the sample check-in, and inspo follows once they say it arrived. Check sample history and videos before including anyone.
- **Follow up after sending inspo:** about 3 days later, in followups.json.

**Replicate every idea for our people (Fatima, 2026-09-24):** for EVERY inspo item, from any source (Atria, TikTok, YouTube, IG, Nivaro, AI-made or real), think it through like a smart human: what makes it work (hook, format, emotion, who is on camera), and how one of OUR creators could film their own version with their real life, kids and setting. Name which creators it fits and why (casting: [[creator-casting-mindset]]), and write the one-line version they would shoot. If an idea can't be replicated safely or believably by anyone on our roster, drop it.
