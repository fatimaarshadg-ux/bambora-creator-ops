**Trybe support answer (Crisp chat, 2026-09-26), use it when a creator asks whether videos get posted to their profile:**
Partnership ads do NOT get posted to the creator's own profile or feed. Running a partnership ad only grants permission to use the creator's Instagram identity, so the ad appears to come from them (their handle and photo show as the sponsor). It does not publish to their profile grid or Stories, so their organic followers do not see it there. The ad only reaches people through Meta's ad delivery and targeting, like any other paid ad.

Creator-friendly version: "no need to post anything! the partnership ads run from your handle but they don't show up on your profile or feed, so your followers won't see them there 😊"

**When unsure about how Trybe works (Fatima, 2026-09-26):** don't guess and don't park it for Fatima. Check Trybe's own material first (portal: "Creators help" / "Discovery help" dropdown top right has guides such as "Program Agreements and the C...", "How to Find & Onboard Creators", "How to Move Creators to Different Programs", plus "Help Center"), then ask Trybe support via "Chat Now" in that same dropdown (Crisp chat). Answers usually come within hours; save useful ones here.
