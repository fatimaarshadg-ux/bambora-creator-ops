On 2026-09-23 Danielle Corbin had written (Sep 22) that "the brief when you click the links you have in there… they aren't working". Fatima had replied "Let me share the revised links". Claude guessed what "the links" meant and sent Danielle a list of old top-performer videos in the thread. Fatima was angry ("I don't know what you're doing") and the message had to be deleted. The real problem was the V3 brief PDF (Bambora-Creator-Invite.pdf), whose video tiles pointed to dead `jump.internal` placeholders. It was fixed and re-rendered on 2026-09-23.

**Why:** she had said "ask me clarifying questions if you don't understand it". A wrong message to a creator costs more than a question to her.

**How to apply:** if a creator refers to something Fatima made or promised (links, a brief, a deal) and it isn't certain which one, ask her in one line, with your best guess and the evidence, before sending anything. Also check tenure: inspo only goes to creators who already have the product ([[inspo-message-rules]]). Related: [[feedback-solve-from-data-first]] (work it out from the data, but ask when the data can't settle it).
