Fatima's criteria for Trybe join requests (Discovery, Inbox), stated 2026-09-23. These are ONLY for deciding whether a new applicant joins the roster. They do NOT apply to reviewing submissions from creators already in the roster; those are judged by [[bambora-content-checklist]].

**Hard requirements (both must be true, otherwise reject):**
- Speaks English.
- Lives in an English-speaking country.

**Then accept if EITHER:**
1. They have yapper-style content (talking to camera, chatty, personality-led), or
2. (see the decision grid below)

**Decision grid (updated 2026-09-23; TikTok bar lowered from 100K likes):**
"Numbers" = **10K+ TikTok likes OR 10K+ TikTok followers OR 10K+ Instagram followers** (any one is enough).
| | Numbers met | Numbers not met |
|---|---|---|
| **Yapper content** | Accept | Energy + 3-4 real comments per recent video, then accept; otherwise reject |
| **No yapper content** | Don't accept blind: DM asking for yapper content, log the follow-up | Reject |
Why she frames it this way: numbers prove people like watching her, but until she's seen the talking-to-camera format from this creator, she's worth asking, not accepting.

**Yapper creators under both thresholds (clarified 2026-09-23):** yapper style alone is not quite enough when numbers are small. They should also have great energy on camera (someone you would love watching) AND at least 3-4 real comments on their recent TikToks (real people reacting, not bots, emoji spam or "follow for follow"). Check the comments on their TikTok, and make the energy call yourself. She said so on 2026-09-23 ("you can do this on your own, I like that you are data driven"). Judge from measurable proxies: face to camera, expression changes across frames, speaking pace (words per second), how lively the wording is, and loudness/pitch variation in the audio. Report the evidence behind each call rather than asking her to verify.

**Automatic reject:** no message sent with the application AND no featured videos in yapper style AND weak engagement on TikTok etc.

**Where to look for yapper-style content (check all of these):**
- the featured videos on their Trybe application card,
- their TikTok and Instagram profiles,
- their application message (Discovery, Messages). Applicants often send a video pitch there, which counts as evidence of yapper style.

**Program:** accept applicants into **Bambora Affiliates V3 (5%) only** (as of 2026-09-23). Do not accept anyone into the 12%, Ambassador or v1 programs; flag V3-less applicants to her.

**Messaging after acceptance:** the automatic V3 welcome is enough. Do NOT draft or send an extra personal DM to newly accepted creators. After accepting the first one, open their thread once and confirm the welcome actually arrived (see [[feedback-test-one-before-batch]]). She has not said whether rejected applicants get any message, so send nothing to them unless she asks.

**Rules from her answers, 2026-09-23:**
- **Yapper definition:** at least part of the video has them speaking straight to camera. Mixing talking-head and b-roll is fine. Pure voiceover over lifestyle shots does NOT count.
- **High numbers but no yapper content:** don't accept or reject yet. DM them asking for yapper content, then log it in [[trybe-applicant-followups]] and follow up the next working day. Template (her voice): "Hey {firstname}, thank you so much for applying with us! Do you have any yapper style / talking head content? We've seen that format do really well for us 💙"
- **Accent/language:** what matters is a clear English accent. A heavily accented English video = reject. If their shared video is in another language: look for English videos in their content. English with a proper accent = approve. None, but real potential (good followers, likes or engagement) = DM: "Hey {firstname}, thank you so much for applying to work with us! Do you have any content in English? Our target demographic is people in English-speaking countries, so we'd love to see some of your videos in English 💙" Log it in [[trybe-applicant-followups]] and check back in a day or two. No potential = reject.
- **Existing Bambora customers** (already own a sling): strong plus, accept.
- **Applicants she has already messaged:** wait for their reply, then judge. Track them in [[trybe-applicant-followups]].
- Every DM still needs her go before sending (sending is irreversible). Batch the drafts and ask once.

**Energy = confident and watchable (her words, 2026-09-23).** Bubbly and loud OR calm and warm are both fine. What fails: doesn't speak well, hesitant, not confident on camera, so you can tell it won't hold attention. Benchmark against her top-performing *yapper* creators (the taste profile at ~/claude-setup/work/trybe-applicant-review/taste-profile.md), not against a generic idea of energy.

**A creator's other content does NOT count against them** (for example "make money on TikTok" videos or unrelated niches), as long as they meet the criteria.

**Ignore GMV.** She does not look at it for applicants.

**Why:** yapper-style talking-to-camera content is what she wants from creators. Audience size is the fallback signal when the style is not proven yet.

**How to apply:** before approving anyone, read their pitch message (Discovery, Messages) and card per the trybe-portal skill, judge against the rules above, and report each verdict with the reason. Cases just under a threshold (for example 9K) go to her as borderline rather than auto-rejected. Never bulk-approve without her go-ahead. Related: [[bambora-content-checklist]], [[bambora-protected-creators]], [[trybe-sample-request-workflow]]

- **Bilingual creators with a clear, nice English accent and yapper content are welcome** (Fatima, 2026-09-23, on Judith Coronel: 3.2K TikTok likes, talks to camera well). Being bilingual never counts against anyone. Under 10K with strong on-camera delivery can still be accepted, so flag these as leaning accept rather than reject.
- **Trybe portfolio overrides weak TikTok engagement (Fatima, 2026-09-23):** if an applicant has a lot of videos on Trybe and they are yapper / talking-head content, low TikTok followers, likes or comments must NOT lead to an automatic reject. Flag them as "worth checking, leaning accept" with a link to their Trybe videos, for her decision. Judith Coronel was the example.
- **Yapper content on the Trybe profile is enough to accept (Fatima, 2026-09-23, on Alison Boutwell):** if an applicant's Trybe featured videos show confident talking-to-camera delivery, accept them. Missing socials, numbers or comment counts are NOT a reason to hold them or ask for socials. Only ask for socials when there's no yapper content anywhere.

## THE deciding rule (Fatima, 2026-09-23; this replaces the numbers-first reading of the grid above)
What decides an applicant is **whether we can see them on camera, talking to camera**, in their Trybe featured videos or their application video.
1. Face on camera + talking to camera + confident, clear delivery (calm or bubbly) + a clear English accent → **ACCEPT**. No socials, follower counts or comment counts needed. Examples: Alison Boutwell, Judith Coronel.
2. Nothing that shows them on camera talking (text and music only, product-only clips, faceless voiceover, lip-sync, no videos at all) → **HOLD / REJECT**, because there's nothing to judge. Example: Jimmy Ralph (one energy drink clip, text and music, no face).
3. Big numbers (10K+ TikTok likes or followers, or 10K+ IG followers) but no on-camera talking anywhere → **DM the yapper ask**.
4. On camera but hesitant, stiff, poorly spoken, or a heavy accent → **REJECT**.
Numbers only come into play when there's no on-camera talking to judge. Never ask for socials from someone whose Trybe videos already show them talking to camera.
