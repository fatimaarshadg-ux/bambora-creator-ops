Creators keep saying the partnership ads request "won't let me click" or "nothing pops up". The request arrives as a card in the Instagram DM from @bamboraco with a **View request** button, and that button frequently does nothing. Telling them to switch to a professional account does not fix it when they already have one. Payton Hoerbert hit this on 2026-09-26 and was still stuck two days later after we gave her the professional-account answer.

**The reliable route, from Instagram's own help centre.** In the Instagram app:

> profile → Menu (top right) → **Professional dashboard** → **Partnership ads** → **Ad partners** → **Pending requests** → Approve

Requirements: a professional account, and content eligible for branded content. Instagram **web** does not support this at all, only the app and Meta Business Suite, which is worth saying if they are trying it on a laptop.

**From our side**, in Meta Business Suite → Creator Marketing → Manage permissions, selecting a partner with "Request sent" gives two buttons worth knowing about:
- **Get link**, which produces a link that drops the creator straight onto the accept screen in the Instagram app. This is the best thing to send someone who is stuck.
- **Notify**, which re-sends the request notification.

Source: Instagram Help Center, "Manage account-level permissions for partnership ads in the Instagram app" (help.instagram.com/742368993017210) and "...in Creator Marketing Hub" (help.instagram.com/966886840676703).

Give the dashboard path first, since it needs nothing from us. Keep **Get link** for anyone still stuck after that.
