Fatima, 2026-09-24: "think of creators in how they can be cast in videos... the sky is really the limit... be very careful about this."

For every creator, ask what roles they can be cast in, then pitch formats that use that role. Always give several varied options, not one obvious idea.
- Pediatrician or nurse: an authority list video recommending several products ("a pediatrician's baby must haves"), with our sling as one pick among honest recommendations. "Things I tell new parents."
- Mom of several kids: "mom of 5 ranks every carrier I've owned", "what I'd buy again for baby #5", "what I wish I knew with my first", sibling-chaos day in the life.
- Grandparent: "what I keep at Grandma's house", the grandparent angle Nivaro runs.
- Also cast from life details, not only job titles: twins, dads, boy moms, working moms, military, rural, bilingual, pregnant (no-baby formats).

**Why:** the right role makes a video believable and gives creators ideas beyond "here's the sling". 
**How to apply:** do it when reviewing applicants (note the castable roles), building creator profiles (`### Style` notes), and writing inspo. Safety rules always apply (10 to 50 lbs, one hand on baby, never "hands free", no medical claims). List lives in ~/claude-setup/work/inspo/authority-creators-*.md. Related: [[inspo-message-rules]], [[core-rules]].

**Roles they don't hold in real life (Fatima, 2026-09-24):** a creator doesn't have to already be an authority figure. Like an actor, they can be cast in a role: an older creator plays a grandparent, a young woman plays the godmother, aunt or babysitter (with a friend's or sister's kid in the 10 to 50 lb range), a mom of one plays "the friend who's done it before". See the potential in each person and stay open-minded; these examples are not the limit. One hard line: no pretend doctors, nurses or other licensed professionals (she said to skip the doctor angle for acted roles). Real professionals can speak as themselves.
