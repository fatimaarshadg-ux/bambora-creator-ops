- **Clear-cut submission approvals:** the video passes all 6 checklist points in every frame, isn't sale-led, and nothing about it is uncertain. Approve, message the creator (inside the send window, [[send-window-us-noon]]), file in Drive, list it under Done. Anything uncertain goes to her.
- **Clear-cut applicants:** the standing accept and reject rules ([[applicant-accept-reject-rule-0927]], [[feedback-auto-accept-applicants]]).
- **Routine actions:** eligible samples, partnership ads requests and nudges, due follow-ups, Drive filing.

She did NOT pick revisions or rejections: those still wait until she has watched the videos herself.

**Why:** she wants the job to run without her reviewing what's obvious, so only real judgement calls reach her.
**How to apply:** same rule on the Mac and on her sister's PC (the handover repo's NEWEST RULES block). When in doubt, it's not clear-cut: bring it to her. Related: [[core-rules]].
