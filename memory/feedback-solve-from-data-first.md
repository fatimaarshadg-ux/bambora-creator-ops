On 2026-09-23 she had to point out that creators with no baby to wear (pregnant, newborns) can film "tester" or "authority" inspo, and that the pack already contained videos with no baby in them. Her words: "one way to do this without needing my hand holding would have been to look at the inspo and see what inspo doesn't have a baby in it."

**Why:** she wants a system that runs her whole creator operation with minimum hand-holding. Every time she corrects something that the data could have answered, the system has failed a little.

**How to apply:** when a creator's situation limits what she can film (no baby, dad, grandparent, no sling yet), look through the available material for what fits before writing anything. Then fix the system so it happens automatically next time: tags, a skill step, a routine step. Only ask her about taste and real decisions.
