## 6. Coverage and caveats

**What is verified:** 11 unique creatives opened and inspected, covering
**~$47.6K of $91.9K lifetime sales (52%)** — including all five of Kia's.

**What is not:** the remaining 195 creatives. The Trybe table paginates 12 at a
time behind an inner scroll and there is no bulk way to see what is *in* a
video, so each one has to be opened by hand.

**Likely also sale-contaminated** — from a transcript scan of the 100 most
recent approved submissions, these creators have ads that *say* the sale out
loud, so their organic numbers are probably lower than they look:

- **Jennifer Thomas** — 5 sale-worded submissions (she is #3 lifetime)
- **katherine bodie** — 2 · **Tasha Clay** — 2 · **Ali Fannin** — 3
- **Ciara Burnett** — 1 · **Danielle Corbin** — 1 · **Marie Barnett** — 1

That list is a starting point, not a verdict — it is transcript-based, and
Kia proves that captions carry promos the transcript never sees.

**Data discrepancy to be aware of:** the Roster header cards say 347 submissions
/ 269 approved, while the "Creators overview" panel directly below says 343 /
267 on the same screen with the same filter. Small, but they disagree.

**Attribution:** "Trybe sales" is Trybe's own attribution. Meta purchase value
($171K) is ~1.9x the Trybe figure, so the two models disagree substantially.
Rankings here use Trybe attribution throughout for consistency.
