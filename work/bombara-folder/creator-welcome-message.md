**Subject line (email): Welcome to Bambora — here's everything you need**

Hey {{first_name}},

Welcome in. You're officially a Bambora creator.

Quick context on who you're making videos for. Bambora makes baby carriers —
**Made by Parents. Loved by Parents.** That isn't a line we bolted on at the end,
it's the reason the product exists. **150,000 happy customers** later, it still
holds: parents buy this because other parents swear by it.

So that's the bar for your videos. Real parents, real homes, real mess. Not a
product demo — you're the 150,001st parent telling someone why it works.

**The deal**

- **5% of GMV** on every sale your content drives, **paid weekly**.
- Your product is on its way — expect it within 1 to 2 weeks.
- Your first month starts the day the product lands on your doorstep, not today.
  You're not on a clock while you wait on a courier.

**What you get from me**

This is the part most brands skip, so read it properly:

- **A weekly drop, every week without fail** — fresh hooks, new inspiration, and
  the real data on what's actually converting right now.
- **Feedback on every single video you submit for your first 30 days.** Notes,
  redos, and the reasoning behind both. Not a thumbs up or a silence.
- **A Calendly link** — book a call with me whenever you want one. You're not
  messaging a support inbox. You're talking to the person running this.
- **A dos-and-don'ts checklist** you fill in before you film — not a long document
  you'll never open.

I don't expect you to arrive as a finished creator. Good creators get trained,
not hired. Bring the effort and I'll bring the direction.

**What happens next**

1. Watch for your product — 1 to 2 weeks.
2. In the meantime, go through the checklist and this week's drop so you're ready
   to film the day it arrives.
3. Submit your first piece inside your first 30 days with the product.
4. Anything at all you're unsure about — message me or book the call. Genuinely.

One last thing. This is the ground floor. Tiers and higher commission are coming
once this first group has product in hand and results on the board — and the
creators who show up properly now are the ones that gets built around.

Really glad you're here.
