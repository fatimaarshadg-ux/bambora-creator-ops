# Authority and persona casting: Bambora creators (2026-09-24)

Read-only research. Nothing was sent or changed on Trybe.

**Sources searched:** creator-db profiles (97), DM threads (96), creators.json, applicant reviews (2026-09-23 and 2026-09-23b: apps.tsv, yap.jsonl transcripts, verdicts), Discovery scratchpad (disc/yap.jsonl, watch transcripts), inspo notes, follow-up ledger, memory (open work). Evidence paths below are relative to `~/claude-setup/work/creator-db/db/` unless shown otherwise.

**Status key:** Protected = the 10 creators on their old commission. V3 = 5% program. V3 new = accepted Sep 23, not yet in the creator DB. Applicant = still pending in the Inbox.

**Safety rules for every idea in this file.** The child is 10 to 50 lbs (never a newborn under 10 lbs). One hand stays on the child in every shot. Seat under the bottom, buckle and safety loop visible. Never say or show "hands free"; the benefit is arm, wrist and back comfort. No medical claims. Real professionals speak only from their own job and stick to how the sling should sit (per the checklist), never claims about hips, development or health outcomes. **No fake credentials:** nobody plays a doctor, nurse, PT or any other licensed role they don't hold.
