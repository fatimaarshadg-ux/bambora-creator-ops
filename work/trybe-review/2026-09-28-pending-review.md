## The pattern worth telling creators

Five of the six trip the same two points: a hand coming off the child while they demonstrate being
hands-free, and burned-in auto-captions. Both are worth a line in the next round of inspo, because
they are cheap to avoid at filming time and expensive to fix afterwards.
