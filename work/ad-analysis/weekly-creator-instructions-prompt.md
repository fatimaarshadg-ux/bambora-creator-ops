# Weekly prompt: top ads into creator instructions

Paste this, attach the week's videos, send. (v2, 2026-09-22: cut to a phone-screen length after the first run came out too long for creators.)

```
These are this week's top performing Bambora ads. For EACH video, watch it fully (transcribe the audio, pull frames, dense in the first 5 seconds) and write instructions I can forward to our Trybe creators.

Hard rule: creators only make top-of-funnel videos. Even if the winning ad is bottom of funnel (offer, sale, founder message, brand name up front), never tell creators to copy that. Find the principle that made it win and show how a creator uses it from her own life: opens on a symptom, scene or story, no brand name in the first 3 seconds, no offer, price, discount or urgency, never posing as founder or staff.

Length: creators read this on a phone between naps. Max 180 words per video. If a line doesn't change what she films, cut it.

Tone: written to the creator, warm, plain, second person, no marketing jargon, nothing that reads as criticism.

One section per video, titled with a short name for the ad, with exactly these subheadings:

## What the ad is
Two sentences max: what happens, and why it worked as a principle she can reuse.

## How to replicate this
One sentence telling her what to do. Then 3 opening lines, each a different device, none copying the original, with the opening shot in brackets where it isn't obvious. Then the rest of the video as ONE line of steps. One filming note at most.

## What not to replicate
Max 5 short bullets. The sales parts first, then only the checklist rules THIS ad actually breaks (don't restate the whole checklist).

Bambora rules to check against (mention only if relevant): seat under the bottom, fabric knee to knee; one hand on baby in every frame, "hands free" is retired, use wrist, arm and back relief; buckle closed over clip with safety loop; baby high enough to kiss, face visible; 10 to 50 lbs as a weight, never an age; no cooking, hot drinks or stairs; vertical 9:16, original file, no captions or watermarks.

After the last video, separately and for me only (not counted in the word limit): stage of awareness and funnel position for each ad, approximate timestamps for any checklist breaks, and one line on this week's pattern.

Never use em dashes or spaced hyphens. If you couldn't see or hear part of a video, say so instead of guessing.
```
