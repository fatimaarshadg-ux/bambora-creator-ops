# The "honest reason" ad

## What the ad is
A founder admits they made too many slings and explains why, over quick clips of real moms. It worked because she was honest about something that cost her, so people believed everything after it.

## How to replicate this
Open by admitting something real from your own life, then say why it happened. Pick one that's true for you:

1. "I've spent over $300 on baby carriers and I use none of them." (tip the pile onto the bed)
2. "I thought my wrist pain was from my phone. It was from my 24-pound daughter."
3. "She asks for 'up' 30 times a day. She's 28 pounds. That's 840 pounds on one arm."

Then: show the real moment, say what you worked out, "so I tried this", two things it fixed for you, end on a nice moment. New shot every 2 to 3 seconds.

## What not to replicate
- No sale, price, discount or "running out".
- Don't speak as the founder, and don't name the brand in the first 3 seconds.
- No hot drinks or stove (both are in this ad). One hand on baby, always.
- Say 10 to 50 lbs, never an age.
- No captions. Send us the clean file.
