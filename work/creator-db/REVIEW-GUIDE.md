# How to fill a creator profile (for Claude and review agents)

Goal (Fatima, 2026-09-23): "know everything about them just as much as I do". Each profile in
`db/profiles/<slug>.md` has an API facts block (do not edit) and a `## Notes` section you fill in.
Never use em dashes or en dashes; use commas, colons or periods. Write plainly, like a brand
manager's notes about a creator she knows well.

## Sources, per creator
1. `db/profiles/<slug>.md`: facts, stats and the transcript of each video (newest first).
2. `db/creators.json`: every submission with status, ads count, review comment, full transcript.
3. `db/media/<slug>/<trybe_id>.jpg`: a 6-frame sheet per video. LOOK AT EVERY ONE (Read tool).
   A missing sheet means the video could not be read; say so.
4. `db/dms/<slug>.md`: DM summary, sample history and the full thread with Fatima.
5. New creators with no videos yet: their applicant review in
   `~/claude-setup/work/trybe-applicant-review/2026-09-23/verdicts.md` (and apps.tsv / yap.jsonl there).
6. Standards: `~/.claude/projects/<PROJECT>/memory/bambora-content-checklist.md` and
   `bambora-sling-safety-rules.md` (seat under the bottom, M position for babies, buckle plus safety loop).
7. This week's inspo pack: `~/claude-setup/work/inspo/2026-09-23/` (order.txt plus why.txt for the 56
   Atria ads in Drive; ytpicks.txt; tt.tsv; ttshop.tsv; ig.tsv). Links doc:
   https://docs.google.com/document/d/1A0xXELm3xo-LnXFCdvAVUjkFQ6F7gDs2vVFZn2nH24A/edit

## What to write under `## Notes` (replace the placeholders, keep any existing human notes)
### Style (from watching her videos)
- Who she is on camera: mom, dad, grandparent, pregnant; baby's age and number of kids if visible;
  setting (home, car, outdoors); look and vibe.
- Format: yapper (talks to camera) or voiceover or text-only; energy (calm, bubbly, flat);
  pacing; typical length; the hooks she opens with (quote them); whether she shows the sling
  being put on, packed, compared.
- What worked: which videos were approved, ran as ads, earned (use the facts); what got rejected
  and why (review comments).
- Safety and checklist issues seen (for example baby slumped, no seat under the bottom).
- Strengths to lean on, and one or two things to improve.
### DMs so far
- Relationship tone, what she asked, what Fatima promised (with dates), open questions or
  unanswered messages, sample status, anything personal that matters (pregnant, twins, newborn).
### Inspo that fits her
- 3 to 5 items from this week's pack that suit HER (style, baby age, setting, format she can pull
  off), each as: source and number or link, then one line on why it fits and what to copy. Never
  her own videos. Prefer non-sale creatives. For protected creators, nothing about commission.
### Quick read
- Two lines at the top of Notes: who she is in one sentence, and the single best next move.
